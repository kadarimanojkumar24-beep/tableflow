TableFlow

QR-based restaurant ordering. Owners register once, then create logins for their waiters and kitchen. Customers only scan the QR code on their table.

Owner / ManagerRegister your restaurant or sign in: tables, QR codes, menu, staff, cashier report Waiter sign inAccept orders, serve, confirm payments Kitchen sign inPrepare orders and mark them ready

Customers: scan the QR code on your table.
